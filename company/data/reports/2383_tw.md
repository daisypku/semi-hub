2026年第一季度毛利率符合摩根大通预期；基础设施业务增长动能保持强劲。EMC一季度毛利率符合我们预期，得益于经营杠杆且无一次性项目，营业利润超我们预期5%；运营支出比率较我们/市场共识低1/1.5个百分点。本次毛利率录得29.4%，较市场共识低1.2个百分点，主要是市场忽略了一个点：高端铜箔基板（CCL）一季度并未启动涨价，因此没有享受到涨价红利（我们预计涨价从5月起正式落地）。营业利润的超预期涨幅，被一次性未分配收益推高的超预期税率抵消了。另一方面，当前AI服务器产品正处于产品迭代过渡期，AI服务器以外的基础设施产品（包括通用服务器、交换机、低轨卫星）共同发力，拉动季度环比营收大增37%。整体来看，我们估算一季度基础设施业务收入占比从2025年第四季度的71%提升至78%。

二季度及2026全年展望：上行风险依然为主。根据我们和管理层的沟通，我们认为二季度营收实现15%-20%的环比增长是完全可以达到的：其中AI服务器需求最强劲，其次是通用服务器；汽车和智能手机需求则会出现个位数的环比下滑。我们预测二季度营收环比增长23%-24%，主要由平均售价（ASP）抬升、产品结构优化、新增产能逐步爬坡共同拉动。关于毛利率展望，我们判断二季度毛利率大概率落在29.5%-32.5%区间，当前价格走势持续走强，因此风险偏向上行（EMC对下半年定价持积极态度）。管理层提到，依托经营杠杆效应，未来运营支出比率将维持平稳或小幅下滑。我们将2026/2027年营业利润预测分别上调10%/13%，主要反映两点变化：1）基础设施（含服务器相关）CCL出货量表现强劲；2）产品结构推动毛利率大幅扩张（预计2026/2027年毛利率分别提升3/7个百分点）——高端基础设施业务的毛利率较公司整体平均水平高5-10个百分点，将占2026/2027年总营收的80%/85%以上。我们给出的2026/2027年营业利润预测，较市场共识分别高出14%/19%。

M9项目更新：项目进度符合计划，EMC的龙头地位将进一步加固。目前仍有投资者质疑今年M9的导入进度，但管理层表示，M9仍按原计划在2026年下半年启动出货。根据我们的行业调研，除了已经确定采用M9的CPX和LPX机架外，英伟达HGX架构的UBB板、Oberon架构的中底板都有可能导入M9；但受产能资源限制，今年下半年大概率只有两款中的一款会采用M9。我们预计2026年M9贡献的收入将占EMC总营收的4%-5%，2027年这一占比将提升至10%。如果Rubin Ultra服务器系列的交换托盘、刀片升级改用M9，那么我们2027年的预测还存在相当可观的上行空间。我们预计EMC在M9项目上将拿下更强的市场份额领先地位：未来两年上市占率将维持80%以上，好于M8项目的表现——我们预计M8可以在1年半的时间里维持70%以上的份额。这主要得益于公司搭配使用Q-glass（石英），且相较于玻纤供应链，EMC和石英布供应商的合作关系要稳固得多。

基材级CCL新机遇浮现，有望推动估值重估。我们将目标价上调至新台币4950元，主要基于两点：1）如上文所述，我们上调了2027年每股收益预期；2）受益于基材级CCL的新机遇，我们将目标市盈率从30倍上调至32倍。这是EMC的全新增量市场，一旦市场意识到该业务在未来12-18个月就能为AI加速器项目贡献可观收入，估值重估就会启动。长期以来，基材材料市场都由少数日本厂商垄断，但我们认为EMC有望打开市场局面，核心原因有两点：1）对比日本供应商普遍偏稳健的扩产节奏，EMC的扩产策略要激进得多；2）EMC有充足的资源研发新型基材级CCL，这类产品采用可替代T玻璃的新型玻璃材料。我们认为Q-glass是非常有竞争力的候选方案：它满足低热膨胀系数（CTE）要求，可适配尺寸不断增大的基材，同时在低介电常数（DK，关乎核心电气性能）这一关键指标上表现优于T玻璃。我们上调后的32倍目标市盈率，较历史平均水平高出两个标准差以上；我们认为这一溢价是合理的，因为EMC新切入基材级CCL市场，这块新市场能给EMC带来30%-40%的增量可触达市场空间。

EMC是印刷电路板（PCB）核心结构材料——覆铜板（CCL）的龙头供应商。我们认为，CCL供应商的行业地位正变得越来越重要，因为这类材料会直接影响数据传输性能；而EMC是经过市场验证的优质厂商，在高性能计算（HPC）领域优势尤其明显。EMC是行业技术龙头，在AI服务器、交换机、卫星等基础设施领域布局深厚，我们认为公司在业务增量和市占率上都拥有很大的成长空间。维持增持评级。我们给出截至2026年12月的目标价为新台币4950元，基于32倍2027年预期每股收益计算。尽管32倍市盈率较历史平均高出两个标准差以上，但我们认为，2026年及之后清晰的业绩催化剂——充分受益于AI强劲需求、在核心GPU/ASIC项目中市占率领先、800G交换机渗透率提升、服务器平台迭代、卫星业务机遇、基材级CCL项目新增量——都将支撑股价，这一估值溢价合情合理。

1Q26 GM in-line w/ JPMe; Infra momentum remains strong. EMC’s 1Q GM was in-line with our expectations while OP beat our expectations by 5% on operating leverage and the absence of one-off items (opex ratio beat JPMe/consensus by 1/1.5ppt). The OP beat was offset by a higher-than-expected tax rate from one-off undistributed earnings. Meanwhile, the strong QoQ rev growth at 37% was driven by a combination of infrastructure product outside AI server, including general server, switch, and LEO, while AI server products are undergoing a product transition. Overall, we estimate that the infrastructure rev mix increased to 78% in 1Q vs. 71% in 4Q25.

2Q and 2026 outlook: risks still on the upside. Based on our conversation with management, we believe 15-20% QoQ rev growth in 2Q is achievable with demand for AI server to be the strongest followed by general server (while both auto and smartphone can see single digit decline QoQ). We forecast 23-24% QoQ growth in 2Q, driven by ASP increase, mix improvement and incremental capacity ramp. For the margin outlook, we believe it is possible to see 2Q GM to be in the 29.5-32.5% range, with risks on the upside on strengthening prices (EMC remains positive on 2H pricing). Management suggested the opex ratio should stay flat or slightly down going forward on operating leverage. We are lifting our 2026/27e OP estimate by 10/13% to reflect 1) strong infrastructure (including server-related) CCL shipments, and 2) strong margin expansion (GM +3/7ppt for 2026/27e) driven by product mix (high-end infrastructure GM could be 5-10 ppt above corporate average, and will account for 80%+/85%+ of overall 2026/27e sales). Our 2026/27e OP is 14/19% above consensus estimates.

M9 update: project on track; EMC could be even more dominating. While some investors are still doubting M9 adoption this year, management suggested that M9 shipment is still on track to take place in 2H26. Based on our industry check, Nvidia HGX structure UBB board/Oberon structure midplane could be the candidates for M9 adoption (but given limited resources, likely one out of two will adopt M9 in 2H), in addition to the confirmed CPX and LPX rack adoptions. We expect M9 revenue to account for 4-5% of EMC rev in 2026e while rising to 10% in 2027e. We believe there’s still decent upside risks to our 2027e estimate if switch tray/blade upgrades to M9 in the Rubin Ultra server series. We expect EMC to deliver even stronger market share leadership in M9 (80%+ m/s for 2+ years) vs. M8 (we expect 70%+ share maintained for 1.5 years) with the inclusion of Q-glass (quartz), as the company enjoys far stronger relations with the quartz cloth suppliers (vs. the glass fiber supply chain).

Substrate CCL opportunity emerging, will likely lead to re-rating. We are lifting TP to NT$4,950 based on 1) higher 2027e EPS estimate (see above), and 2) lifting target P/E to 32x (was 30x) on emerging substrate CCL opportunity. As this is a new TAM for EMC, re-rating will likely take place once the market realizes its potential to see meaningful revenue contribution from AI accelerator projects in the coming 12-18 months. For years the substrate material market has been dominated by selective Japanese players. However, we believe the door could open for EMC on 1) the company’s much more aggressive strategy in building capacity vs. the typical moderate pace among Japanese suppliers, and 2) the company could possess the needed resources to develop new substrate CCL, which is based on alternative glass materials to T-glass. We believe Q-glass is an intriguing candidate as it also meets the low CTE requirement (thermal expansion resistant which supports increasingly larger substrate body size) while outperforming T-glass on the low-DK metric (related to the critical electrical performance). Our new target multiple of 32x (was 30x) is over 2-STD above the historical average, which we think is justified by its emerging entry into the substrate CCL market, which is an additional 30-40% TAM to EMC.

EMC is a leading supplier of laminates, the main construction material for PCBs. We believe the role of copper clad laminate (CCL) suppliers is becoming more important, as the material could have a strong effect on data transmission performance, and that EMC is a proven quality player, especially for the HPC segment.
